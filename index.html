How it works

  1. Staff sign up

    Teachers and staff who want to join share a few fun facts about themselves. It only takes a few minutes.

  2. I design the cards

    I turn those fun facts into colorful, one-of-a-kind cards for each staff member.

  3. Your school sells them

    Your school or PTA sells the cards to families and keeps a share of the money raised.

Built with care

  • Consent from every staff member

    No one appears on a card unless they've said yes first.

  • School-appropriate

    Every card is friendly, positive, and right for a school setting.

  • No student photos

    Cards feature staff only. Student photos are never used.
